Personal Learning Record

A log of key subjects I have been studying since starting my degree, including the difficulties I faced and how I worked through them.

1. Weekly C Programming Labs & Debugging

Fall 2025 – Present

What I learned: Basic C syntax, variables, loops, conditional statements, functions, and how to trace errors in small console programs.

Problem encountered: At first, syntax errors, missing semicolons, and understanding why a loop produced the wrong output made debugging slow and frustrating.

How it was solved: Practicing every week during lab sessions, breaking small tasks into steps, and testing outputs line by line. I am still improving my ability to write cleaner logic independently without relying too much on hints.

2. Calculus & College Physics Adaptation

2025 – 2026

What I learned: Core limits, derivatives, and foundational physics formulas required for first-year engineering coursework.

Problem encountered: Transitioning into university-level Calculus and Physics in an international environment was challenging at the start, especially keeping up with new terminology and problem-solving speed.

How it was solved: Studying regularly with friends, solving extra practice exercises, memorizing key formulas, and watching supplementary explanation videos in Russian to reinforce difficult concepts.

3. Linear Algebra Foundations

2026

What I learned: Matrix operations, systems of linear equations, determinants, and vector basics.

Problem encountered: Multi-step matrix calculations often led to small arithmetic mistakes that ruined the final answer.

How it was solved: Writing out intermediate steps neatly instead of skipping lines, double-checking row operations, and reviewing solutions with classmates.
